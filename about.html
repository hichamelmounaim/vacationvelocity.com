About Vacation Velocity

We believe that you don't need to quit your job to see the world. You just need to travel faster.

Our Mission

Vacation Velocity was created for the modern traveler: the professional with limited PTO, the weekend warrior, the person who wants to see the world without upending their life. Our mission is to provide hyper-efficient, action-packed 48-hour itineraries that eliminate the guesswork from short trips.

Why Fast Travel?

The traditional travel industry assumes you have two weeks to slowly meander through a country. But most of us don't. We have Friday night to Sunday night. We realized that by optimizing packing, mastering budget airlines, and knowing exactly what to do when you land, a weekend getaway can feel like a full vacation.

  • No Wasted Time: Our itineraries skip the tourist traps and get straight to the best parts of a city.
  • Carry-on Only: We advocate for ultra-light packing to avoid baggage fees and carousel waits.
  • Budget Smart: Fast travel doesn't mean expensive travel. We show you how to maximize your dollar.

Our Tools

Beyond our travel guides, we build practical tools like our Travel Budget Estimator to help you plan trips efficiently. We want you spending less time on spreadsheets and more time exploring.
